# 🚀 AI-Powered UGC Ad Content System

**Author:** V Mouli

**Project:** Prompt Engineering Task 2 (Future Interns) 

**Niche/Business:** Midnight Maggi & Munchies (Late-Night Campus Food Delivery)  

## 📌 Project Overview
Today’s college students ignore polished, corporate commercials. They buy from authentic, highly relatable User-Generated Content (UGC). This project demonstrates a systematized AI prompt engineering workflow to generate high-converting UGC ad scripts for **Midnight Maggi & Munchies**, a hyper-local late-night food delivery service for college hostels.

The goal of this system is to bridge the gap between AI generation and real-world marketing by creating content that is ready for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and campus WhatsApp groups.

---

## 🛠️ The Prompt Engineering System

Below are the exact AI prompts used to generate the content. I used [ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini] for this workflow.

### Prompt 1: The Persona Setup (System Prompt)
> "Act as a Senior UGC Ad Strategist. Your goal is to write raw, authentic short-form ad scripts that don't sound like ads. Our business is 'Midnight Maggi & Munchies', a hyper-local campus food delivery service that delivers hot snacks (Maggi, fries, cold coffee) directly to college hostels between 11 PM and 4 AM. Our target audience is college students cramming for exams or hanging out late. Keep the tone casual, relatable, and native to Instagram Reels/TikTok. Do not use corporate marketing buzzwords."

### Prompt 2: The Hook Generator
> "Generate 3 distinct, scroll-stopping hooks for this food delivery service. Use these angles: 
> 1. A relatable 'late-night study' POV. 
> 2. A dramatic/funny hook about terrible hostel food. 
> 3. A 'secret hack' hook for campus life.
> Provide both the visual action and the spoken audio."

### Prompt 3: The Script Builder
> "Take the 'Late-Night Study' hook and build a full 20-second UGC script around it. Use the 'Problem-Agitation-Solution-CTA' framework. Format the output with clear timestamps, visual instructions for the creator, and the exact spoken dialogue."

## 📈 The Generated Output (Content Pack)

### 1. The Hooks Library
* **Hook 1 (The Study POV):** "POV: It’s 2 AM, you have a massive submission tomorrow, and all you have left is dry biscuits." *(Visual: Student looking exhausted at a laptop, panning to a sad packet of empty biscuits).*
* **Hook 2 (The Hostel Joke):** "I love our campus, but if I have to eat the hostel mess food one more time, I'm dropping out." *(Visual: Creator holding a plate of unappetizing food, looking directly into the camera in despair).*
* **Hook 3 (The Hack):** "Freshmen, listen up. Here is the ultimate campus cheat code for when the canteen closes at 10 PM." *(Visual: Creator whispering to the camera like they are sharing a secret).*

### 2. High-Converting Script Example (The "Late Night Savior" Angle)
**Length:** 20 Seconds  
**Format:** Face-to-camera / Relatable Vlog  

* **[0:00-0:03] Visual:** Creator looking exhausted at their desk, surrounded by notes. Then, their phone lights up with a text.
* **[0:00-0:03] Audio:** "Nothing hits worse than that 2 AM hunger when every single place on campus is closed."
* **[0:04-0:09] Visual:** Screen recording of quickly messaging an order on WhatsApp or a simple app, then a shot of walking down the hostel stairs.
* **[0:04-0:09] Audio:** "I used to just go to sleep hungry, but I finally found out about Midnight Munchies."
* **[0:10-0:15] Visual:** Creator back at their desk, taking a massive, steaming bite of hot Maggi or cheesy fries. They look incredibly happy.
* **[0:10-0:15] Audio:** "They literally deliver hot food right to the hostel gate in 15 minutes. It’s actually saving my life right now."
* **[0:16-0:20] Visual:** Creator pointing down. Text overlay: "Save this for your next all-nighter. Link in bio to order." 
* **[0:16-0:20] Audio:** "If you're studying late tonight, do yourself a favor. Link in my bio to get your food delivered right now."

## 🧠 Why This Works (Analysis)
* **Hyper-Targeted Pain Points:** Every college student relates to the feeling of 2 AM hunger combined with academic stress. It immediately builds empathy.
* **Visual Proof:** Showing the hot food after the "struggle" of studying provides a satisfying visual resolution.
* **Frictionless CTA:** The call-to-action specifically targets the immediate pain point ("If you're studying late tonight... order right now"), creating urgency without sounding like a traditional salesperson.
